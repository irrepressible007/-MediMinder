## MediMinder is a reminder, not a doctor

- MediMinder helps you remember medicines, health checks and habits. It does not give medical advice, diagnose or treat anything.
- Always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions. Do not start, stop or change a medicine because of something the app shows.
- Labels such as "normal" or "high" use general adult ranges. Your doctor may set different targets for you.
- In an emergency, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital.

## Reminders can fail

Phones can delay or stop reminders: battery savers, switched-off notifications, a phone that is switched off, or a wrong clock. The app does its best and warns you about settings that stop reminders, but it cannot promise that every reminder will ring. Do not rely on MediMinder alone for medicines where a missed or late dose could be dangerous.

## Medicine information

The medicine list comes from public sources, including the Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA) product lists. Names, strengths and prices can be out of date or wrong. Check with your pharmacist. Reading boxes and prescriptions with the camera can make mistakes, so always check what was found before saving it.
