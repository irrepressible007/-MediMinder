# MediMinder Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

MediMinder is a free medicine-reminder app for Android, made by an independent developer (irrepressible007). This policy explains what the app keeps, where, and what choices you have. In short: your health information stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up, sign in, or share it.

## What stays on your phone

- Your medicines, dose times, what you took or skipped, and stock counts
- Health readings (blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, temperature, oxygen) and habits
- Doctor visits, your Medical ID (blood group, allergies, conditions, emergency contact) and the documents you add
- Photos of medicine boxes or strips that you choose to take
- Your settings, and a problem log of app errors (no health details are written to it)

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Nobody else, including the developer, can see it.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when you do one of these:

- **Cloud backup (optional).** If you sign in with Google or email, a copy of your MediMinder data is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, in the Mumbai region) so it can come back after you reinstall or change phones. It includes the same things as a backup file: medicines, history, readings, doctor visits, Medical ID and settings. Photos are not uploaded. Your copy can only be read by your own account. Google provides this service on our behalf.
- **Signing in.** The sign-in service you choose (Google, or Firebase for email) tells MediMinder your account ID, name and email address. MediMinder never posts anything on your behalf.
- **Text message to your family (optional).** If you turn this on, when a medicine dose is missed the app sends a normal SMS from your phone to the number you chose. It contains your name, the medicine names and their times. Your mobile operator delivers it and may charge for it.
- **Sharing.** When you share a report, a PDF or a backup file, it goes wherever you send it.
- **Your phone's own backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include MediMinder's data in your Google account's device backup, so it can come back on a new phone. Google encrypts this backup with your screen lock. You can turn it off in the phone's settings.

## Things done on the phone

- **Reading boxes and prescriptions.** Text in camera photos is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit. The photos are not sent to the developer. A prescription photo is deleted once it has been read. Google may collect basic usage statistics about ML Kit, as described in Google's terms.
- **Update checks.** The app checks GitHub for a newer version. As with any web request, GitHub can see your internet address. No personal information is sent.

## What MediMinder does not do

- No advertising, no tracking and no analytics
- Your information is never sold or shared for marketing
- The developer cannot read your health information

## Permissions

Notifications and alarms (to remind you on time), camera (to read boxes and prescriptions; only when you use it), SMS (only if you turn on family texts), and internet (for updates and the optional cloud backup).

## Your choices

- **Delete everything on the phone:** uninstall MediMinder, or clear its storage in the phone's settings.
- **Delete your cloud copy and account:** in the app, open More → Account → Delete my account and cloud data. This removes your cloud copy and your sign-in account straight away. If you can no longer use the app, ask through the contact below and it will be done within 30 days.
- **Take your data with you:** More → Settings → Backup makes a file you can keep or move.

## Security

Information on your phone is protected by Android's app storage. The cloud copy is sent over encrypted connections and stored encrypted by Google, and access rules let only your account read it. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone and sign-in account protected.

## Children

MediMinder is not meant for children under 13 to use on their own. A parent or carer may use it to manage a child's medicines.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date, and the app will show it.
